## 1. Introduction

1.1 **Purpose.** This policy sets out the rules for accessing and using Company IT systems, devices, data and communication tools, and for reporting IT security incidents.

1.2 **Scope.** It applies to all Company-owned devices (laptops, desktops, phones, handheld scanners, trackers and dashcams), Company systems and accounts, and to personal devices used to access Company email or data.

1.3 **Related documents**
- IFL-HR-001 HR & Leave Policy (probation, clearance on exit)
- IFL-HR-002 Code of Conduct (confidentiality, social media, discipline)
- IFL-OPS-003 Driver Handbook (phone use while driving, tracker and dashcam)
- IFL-SAF-005 Accident & Incident Reporting Procedure (physical incidents; data retention)

1.4 **IT contacts.** IT Helpdesk: ext. 130, itsupport@indusfreight.example. The IT Support Officer – Lahore covers the warehouse, fleet yard and Control Room. Outside office hours, drivers and warehouse staff report urgent IT issues through the Control Room (ext. 222), which contacts the on-call IT Support Officer.

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |

| System owner | The manager responsible for approving access to a system. |

## 3. Systems and Access

3.1 **Company systems and owners**

**Table 3.1 – Systems, owners and access approval**

| System | Used for | System owner (approves access) |
|---|---|---|
| Email and calendar | Company communication | IT Manager |
| Transport Management System (TMS) | Trip planning, dispatch, trip sheets | Operations Manager |
| Warehouse Management System (WMS) | Stock, bin locations, picking | Warehouse Manager |
| Telematics portal | Trackers, dashcams, speed alerts | Fleet Manager |
| Delivery App | Proof of delivery (LTV drivers) | Operations Manager |
| Finance / ERP system | Accounts, payments, expense claims | Finance Manager |
| HRIS | Leave, attendance, personal records | HR Manager |
| Shared drive | Departmental files | Department manager of each folder |

3.2 **Requesting access**
- 3.2.1 Access is requested on Form IT-01 (System Access Request). It must be approved by the employee's line manager and then by the system owner.
- 3.2.2 IT grants approved access within 2 working days.
- 3.2.3 Access is given on a least-privilege basis: only what is needed for the job.

3.3 **Access reviews.** System owners review user access:
- every 3 months for the Finance/ERP system and HRIS;
- every 6 months for all other systems.

3.4 **Probationers.** Probationary employees receive the system access their role needs. However, they are not given VPN or remote access until they are confirmed, unless the COO approves an exception in writing.

3.5 **Transfers.** When an employee changes role or location, the new line manager submits a new IT-01 within 5 working days. Access not needed for the new role is removed.

3.6 **Leavers**
- 3.6.1 HR informs IT of the last working day at least 5 working days in advance.
- 3.6.2 All access is disabled by 17:30 on the last working day.
- 3.6.3 For dismissals, access is disabled immediately when the employee is informed.
- 3.6.4 The leaver's mailbox is kept for 90 days and may be accessed by the line manager with HR approval.

## 4. Passwords and Authentication

4.1 **Password rules**
- 4.1.1 Passwords must be at least 12 characters and must not include the employee's name, CNIC number, or the word "indus".
- 4.1.2 Passwords for the Finance/ERP system and HRIS must be changed every 90 days. Passwords for other systems are changed only when compromise is suspected or when IT requires it.
- 4.1.3 Passwords must never be shared, written down near the device, or sent by email or messaging app. IT staff will never ask for a password.

4.2 **Account lockout.** Accounts lock after 5 failed login attempts. They unlock automatically after 30 minutes, or sooner through the IT Helpdesk.

4.3 **MFA.** MFA is required for:
- email accessed from outside Company offices;
- VPN connections;
- the Finance/ERP system and HRIS from any location.

4.4 **Shared accounts**
- 4.4.1 Shared or generic accounts are not allowed.
- 4.4.2 **Exception:** the Control Room console uses one shift account per console, because it must stay logged in across shift changes. Each Dispatch Supervisor or controller must record their name and the start and end time of their shift in the console shift log. The shift account password is changed whenever a Control Room staff member leaves.

4.5 **Delivery App.** LTV drivers sign in with their employee number and a 6-digit PIN. PINs must not be shared with helpers or customers.

| G4–G5 | ✔ | ✔ (smartphone) | — |
| G3 (all) | ✔ | ✔ (Control Room, Fleet and Warehouse Supervisors only) | — |
| G2 office roles (Admin Officers, Customer Service Officers) | Desktop | — | — |

| LTV drivers | — | ✔ (smartphone with Delivery App only) | Tracker + dashcam in vehicle |
| Warehouse staff | — | — | Shared handheld scanners (12 units) |

5.2 **Issue and return**
- 5.2.1 Every device is issued and returned using Form IT-02 (Device Issue/Return).
- 5.2.2 Handheld scanners are signed out at the shift desk at the start of each shift and returned at the end. They are not recorded on IT-02 for each shift.
- 5.2.3 Devices must be returned before final settlement (see IFL-HR-001 §11.4).

5.3 **Care of devices**
- 5.3.1 Devices must be locked when unattended (Windows key + L on laptops).
- 5.3.2 Laptops must not be left in vehicles overnight or visible in a parked vehicle.
- 5.3.3 Laptops must be carried as hand luggage when flying, never in checked baggage.

5.4 **Lost or stolen devices**
- 5.4.1 Report to the IT Helpdesk (or the Control Room outside office hours) within 2 hours of discovering the loss, so the device can be locked or wiped.
- 5.4.2 Complete Form IT-03 within 24 hours. For theft, a police report is also required within 3 working days.
- 5.4.3 There is no charge for a first loss reported on time.
- 5.4.4 For a second loss within 24 months, or a loss caused by clear negligence, 25% of the device's depreciated value is recovered from salary, up to a maximum of PKR 30,000. Recovery follows the monthly limit in IFL-HR-002 §9.2.2.

5.5 **Software.** Only IT may install software. Requests are made to the IT Helpdesk with line manager approval. Unlicensed or pirated software is prohibited.

5.6 **USB storage.** USB storage devices are blocked on Company laptops. Encrypted Company USB drives may be issued with IT Manager approval, for a stated business purpose.

## 6. Acceptable Use

6.1 **Personal use.** Limited personal use of Company email, internet and phones is allowed if it does not interfere with work, does not cost the Company extra, and follows this policy.

6.2 **Prohibited uses.** Employees must not use Company systems or devices to:
- access, store or share pornographic, violent, hateful or extremist material;
- gamble, trade cryptocurrency or run a private business;
- download films, music or games;
- harass, threaten or insult anyone (see IFL-HR-002 §4.2);
- bypass security controls, filtering or monitoring;
- send Company data to personal email accounts or personal cloud storage;
- use AI tools or online services not approved by IT to process customer or Restricted data.

6.3 **Approved AI tools.** Employees may use AI tools approved by the IT Manager for drafting and summarising Internal or Public data only. Confidential and Restricted data must not be entered into any AI tool unless the IT Manager has approved that specific use in writing.

6.4 **Company phones**
- 6.4.1 Driver phones are for work calls only. Personal calls on driver phones are limited to emergencies.
- 6.4.2 Phone use while driving is governed by IFL-OPS-003 §3.3.
- 6.4.3 Company phone bills above the plan limit caused by personal use are recovered from the employee.

6.5 **Personal devices**
- 6.5.1 G3–G5 employees may access Company email on a personal phone if they register it with IT, use MFA and accept the Company's device management profile. IT may remotely wipe Company data (not personal data) from the device if it is lost or the employee leaves.
- 6.5.2 Drivers must not use personal phones for the Delivery App or to photograph customer documents.

## 7. Email, Messaging and Social Media

7.1 **Email.** Company email is the official channel for customer and vendor communication. Contracts, rates, invoices and customer documents must be sent by Company email, not by messaging apps.

7.2 **Messaging apps**
- 7.2.1 Operational messaging groups (for example, dispatch and shift groups) may be used for quick coordination. They must be created by a supervisor, and the supervisor must remove members who leave the team.
- 7.2.2 Messaging groups must not be used to share CNIC copies, salary details, medical information, customer rate sheets or full consignment documents.
- 7.2.3 Photos of delivery proof must be taken in the Delivery App, not shared in groups.

7.3 **Social media**
- 7.3.1 Employees must not post photos of customer consignments, vehicles carrying customer goods, warehouse interiors, the Control Room screens or Company documents (see IFL-HR-002 §7.4).
- 7.3.2 Employees must not claim to speak for the Company or comment on accidents, customers or competitors (see IFL-HR-002 §7.6).
- 7.3.3 Employees may mention that they work for the Company in their profiles.

7.4 **Phishing.** Employees must not click links or open attachments in unexpected messages. Requests to change a vendor's bank details, or urgent payment requests, must always be verified by phone using a number already on file, never a number given in the message.

## 8. IT Security Incidents

8.1 **What to report.** Examples include:
- clicking a link or opening an attachment in a suspected phishing email;
- entering a password on a suspicious website;
- a lost or stolen device (§5.4);
- unusual account activity or unexpected password reset messages;
- data sent to the wrong person;
- malware warnings;
- tracker or dashcam tampering or loss of signal for more than 30 minutes on a trip.

8.2 **How and when**
- 8.2.1 Report to the IT Helpdesk (ext. 130) immediately, and no later than 1 hour after noticing the incident. Outside office hours, report through the Control Room (ext. 222).
- 8.2.2 Complete Form IT-03 (IT Security Incident Report) within 24 hours.
- 8.2.3 Do not try to investigate or fix the incident yourself, delete emails, or turn off the device unless IT tells you to.

8.3 **Escalation**
- 8.3.1 The IT Manager informs the COO within 4 hours if an incident involves customer data, Restricted data, or a payment.
- 8.3.2 The Commercial Manager informs affected customers where required, after COO approval.
- 8.3.3 If a tracker signal is lost during a trip, the Control Room first follows the vehicle security steps in IFL-OPS-003 §8.3, and reports it as an IT incident afterwards.

8.4 **Physical harm.** If an IT incident also involves injury, vehicle theft or cargo loss, it is reported under IFL-SAF-005 as well.

8.5 **No blame for prompt reporting.** Employees who report promptly that they clicked a phishing link are not disciplined for the click itself. Failing to report, or hiding an incident, is a disciplinary matter under IFL-HR-002 §9.

## 9. Data Classification and Handling

9.1 **Classes**

**Table 9.1 – Data classification and handling**

| Class | Examples | Email | Messaging apps | Printing | Storage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Public | Published service brochures, website text | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | Any Company location |
| Internal | Policies, rosters, route plans | ✔ (internal) | ✔ (operational groups) | ✔ | Shared drive |

9.2 **Data retention.** Data is kept for the periods in the Company retention schedule maintained by the IT Manager. Incident-related records are kept as set out in IFL-SAF-005 §10.1.

9.3 **Disposal.** Paper containing Confidential or Restricted data is shredded. Devices are wiped by IT before reuse or disposal.

## 10. Monitoring and Privacy

10.1 **What is monitored.** The Company may monitor the use of its systems and devices, including:
- email and internet logs;
- system access logs;
- vehicle tracker data (location, speed, harsh braking);
- dashcam footage.

10.2 **Dashcam footage**
- 10.2.1 Footage is reviewed only for incidents, customer or public complaints, investigations, and a monthly random audit of 5% of trips by the Fleet Supervisor.
- 10.2.2 Access is limited to the Fleet Manager, Fleet Supervisor, HSE Manager and investigators appointed under IFL-SAF-005 §8.

10.3 **Retention of telematics data**

| Data | Normal retention | When linked to an incident |
|---|---|---|
| Dashcam footage | 30 days (overwritten automatically) | Downloaded within 24 hours and kept as per IFL-SAF-005 §10.1.3 |
| Tracker data | 12 months | As per IFL-SAF-005 §10.1.3 |
| Email | 5 years | Until the matter is closed |

10.4 **Personal privacy.** Monitoring is used for business, safety and security purposes only. Personal content found during monitoring is not used unless it shows a breach of Company policy.

## 11. Remote Work and Travel

11.1 Remote work is allowed only for head office G3–G5 staff with line manager approval, and only for confirmed employees (see §3.4).

11.2 Remote access to Company systems must use the VPN with MFA. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN switched on.

11.3 Confidential or Restricted documents must not be printed at home or in public print shops.

## 12. Breaches

12.1 Breaches of this policy are handled under IFL-HR-002 §9.

12.2 The following are treated as gross misconduct under IFL-HR-002 §9.4:
- deliberately disabling trackers, dashcams or security software;
- sharing Restricted data outside the Company without authority;
- using another person's account to commit fraud.
